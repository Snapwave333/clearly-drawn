Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. The short version

This website is a static page. It does not set cookies, it does not run analytics, and it does not track you. The only personal data Clearly Drawn handles is the data TikTok gives us about the account you deliberately connect, plus the record of the videos we published to it.

2. What this website collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up form, no analytics script, no advertising tag and no cookie on any page of this site. When you click "Connect TikTok", your browser leaves this site and goes to TikTok; from that point TikTok's own privacy policy applies to what you see and approve.

3. What the publishing service stores

When you connect a TikTok account, TikTok redirects back to this site with an authorisation code. The publishing service exchanges that code for a refresh token and stores it as a secret. Stored alongside it are:

a refresh token          what lets the service request short-lived access tokens for your account
your TikTok user id       which account the token belongs to
your public profile       the name and avatar you granted user.info.basic and user.info.profile for
a publish log             for each video we published: the time, the video URL, and the link TikTok returned

4. What the service does not store

The client secret and the refresh token are never written into this website, into any page you can view, or into the publish log. The service does not store your TikTok password, does not read your direct messages, drafts you have not published, or anything outside the scopes you granted.

5. Why we are allowed to hold it

The refresh token is held on the legal basis that you gave it to us, deliberately, to perform the publishing you asked for. We hold it only for as long as you keep the account connected.

6. Who else sees it

TikTok does, because the tokens and the videos pass through TikTok's API — that is the point of the integration. Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or share this data with any third party, and there is no advertising or analytics recipient to disclose.

7. How long we keep it

The refresh token is kept until you revoke access or the service stops running. The publish log is kept as an operational record of what was published to the account. If you want any of it deleted, ask, and we will delete it — the token can be revoked instantly, and it takes effect immediately.

8. How to take it back

Two routes, both immediate:

TikTok app:    Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Account → Apps and websites
               → find Clearly Drawn → remove
Publishing host: tiktok-mcp auth --revoke

Revoking stops every future post straight away. It does not delete videos that were already published, because those now live in your account on TikTok's own terms.

9. AI-generated content

Where TikTok requires AI-generated content to be declared, the publishing service sets the AI-generated flag on the post and you are shown it before confirming. We do not present generated footage as footage of real events.

10. Security

The secret lives in the operator's secret store, not in any file this website can serve. Access tokens are short-lived, requested only when a publish runs, and discarded afterwards. The code that runs the publishing pipeline is stored with the rest of the project's source and keeps secrets out of it.

11. Children

The service is not directed at children, and a TikTok account belongs to whoever created it. If you believe a child's account has been connected without permission, revoke access and tell us.

12. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date at the top is the current version. Questions about it can be raised through the contact route published on this site.
