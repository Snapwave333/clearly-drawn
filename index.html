Clearly Drawn

An internal publishing tool that takes a finished short video, connects a TikTok account once, and uploads the video through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Login Kit Content Posting API Direct Post No third-party uploaders

Clearly Drawn automates one narrow job: moving a rendered whiteboard short from the render queue to TikTok, with a caption, without anyone opening the TikTok app. Everything a creator normally does by hand — picking the file, writing the caption, choosing who can see it, waiting for the post to go live — happens through TikTok's own documented API, using credentials the creator granted explicitly.

How it works

  1. You connect your TikTok account once. The button on this page sends you to TikTok's own authorisation screen. You choose which permissions to grant and approve them there — nothing is typed into TikTok on your behalf by anyone else.
  2. The publishing service keeps a refresh token. TikTok returns a refresh token to the publishing service, which stores it as a secret and uses it to request short-lived access tokens. The token is never shown to any third party.
  3. New videos are posted automatically. When a video finishes rendering, the service uploads the file to TikTok, attaches the caption, waits for TikTok to confirm the post is live, and records the resulting link.

What the tool can and cannot do

Posts to your own accountOnly the account that granted permission. The tool cannot post anywhere else.
Uploads files you already haveIt sends a video file that has already been rendered and stored on a verified host.
Nothing without your approvalEvery run starts with confirmation. Nothing is published silently.
Cannot read your drafts or DMsOnly the scopes granted at the authorisation screen are used, and only for publishing.

Opens tiktok.com/v2/auth/authorize with the scopes this app requests. You approve or deny on TikTok's screen, and TikTok returns to callback.html on this same domain.
