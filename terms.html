1. What these terms cover

These terms govern your use of Clearly Drawn, a publishing tool that uploads already-rendered short videos to a TikTok account you own or control, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. By connecting a TikTok account to Clearly Drawn, or by using the tool to publish a video, you agree to these terms.

2. What the tool does

Clearly Drawn does three things. It sends you to TikTok's authorisation screen so you can grant permissions to your own account. It stores the refresh token TikTok issues to your account, as a secret on the operator's own infrastructure. And, when you confirm a publish run, it uploads the video file you point it at, attaches the caption you supply, and records the link TikTok returns.

3. Permissions you grant

You grant exactly the permissions you tick on TikTok's authorisation screen, and nothing else. The tool requests the following scopes and uses them only for the purposes listed:

user.info.basic       read your public profile name and avatar, to show which account is connected
user.info.profile    read your public profile details, to display the connected account
user.info.stats      read your public follower and like counts, for the reports you request
video.list           list your existing public posts, so you can see what has already been published
video.upload         upload a video file to your account's draft inbox or as a direct post
video.publish        publish a video to your account at the visibility you chose

You can withdraw any of these permissions at any time, and you can revoke access entirely.

Clearly Drawn does not post to anyone else's account. It does not buy engagement, follow or unfollow accounts, send direct messages, comment, or interact with other users. It does not bypass TikTok's rate limits, its private-post requirement for unreviewed apps, or any part of TikTok's platform rules.
